# Editorial university design
Reference: https://www.stanford.edu/ inspected 15 November 2026 using Firecrawl branding/markdown and a Chrome screenshot at 1440×1000 (local reference /private/tmp/stanford-reference.png).

Observed: two compact header rows; full-width photographic/video opening; large white serif headline near the lower left; dark lower image overlay; restrained red navigation. Content is organized by academics, research and audience. Firecrawl reports Edict/Georgia heading stack, Source Sans 3 body, cardinal #8C1515, white background, largely square surfaces. Its sampled 20px h1 is not representative of the visible hero and is not used. Full-page and mobile reference layouts were not visually verified.

Adaptation, not reproduction: retain Unpad logo, existing type for body, deep teal #153c43, gold #ae812e, warm paper #f5f3ee. Georgia headings (no proprietary fonts), 40–76px responsive hero, 30–48px section headings, generous 48–72px section spacing. These are proposed values. Use existing supplied community photographs in the existing accessible slideshow. No Stanford image or branding is copied.

Place existing program cards below the photographic hero; use white bordered cards, top accent rules, clear text and solid action buttons. Six audience shortcuts share one orderly grid. Add compact program/research/service navigation with existing routing attributes. Preserve every original link, ID, dataset and panel, including emphatic survey and tracer invitations. Maintain reduced-motion behavior, keyboard focus, language switch and mobile menu.
